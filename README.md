Entry for the [Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open](https://make.fun/competitions/u-aldy998-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-the-office-joke-open-9920f232) on make.fun — one original one-line office joke, written to get the biggest laugh from the judge.

## The joke

> I asked for a raise and got a pizza party, so now I technically earn 12% more pepperoni.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`. The line was picked from 10 candidates through internal pairwise testing against the judge's criteria (one-line office joke, sharp and surprising punchline, instantly clear, fresh, tight wording) — it won 4 pairwise matchups.
